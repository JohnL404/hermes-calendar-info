Your calendar, through a private conversation.

Hermes Calendar is a personal Google Calendar integration operated by JohnL404. It is designed to find and summarize events through a private encrypted Matrix conversation and prepare event changes for the owner's review.

Calendar access

After the owner authorizes Google access, Hermes can discover subscribed calendars and read events wherever the connected account has permission. Creation and updates require an exact proposal followed by a new approval from the verified owner. The integration exposes no event deletion, calendar creation, sharing, or guest-management tools.

Private service, public information

This website provides information and the privacy policy. It offers no sign-in, public assistant, or calendar interface. Private authorization and calendar processing take place separately from this site. Calendar connection is being prepared; live validation is pending.

Support

The authorized owner can request support or removal of private records through the existing private Hermes conversation. Operator: JohnL404.
